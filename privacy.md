# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 28, 2026_

HK Playground (`zayunsna.github.io`) is a personal technical blog. This page explains what information
is collected when you visit it, and by whom. I do not run my own analytics, I do not ask you to create
an account, and I do not sell or share any personal data.

## Hosting

This site is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/). When you visit, GitHub may log
technical information such as your IP address for security and operational purposes.
See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Advertising (Google AdSense)

This site uses Google AdSense to display ads.

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this
  website or other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your
  visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
- You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).
  You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at
  [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

For details, see [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Comments (utterances)

Post comments are powered by [utterances](https://utteranc.es/), which stores each comment as an issue
in a public GitHub repository. To comment you sign in with your GitHub account, and your comment,
GitHub username, and avatar become publicly visible. This is governed by GitHub's privacy statement above.
Reading comments does not require signing in.

## Fonts

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser sends a request (including your IP address) to
Google's servers. See the [Google Fonts privacy FAQ](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy).
